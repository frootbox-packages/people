**People** keeps your team and your locations in one place. The
website shows them with three blocks: **Team**, **Contact person** and
**Location**. A change here appears on every page that shows the person
or the location.

## People

Choose **New person** and enter the name and, if you like, what the
person does. On the next screen you add a title such as "Dr.", the
email address, the phone number, a photo, the locations the person
works at and a few sentences about the person. **Order in the team**
puts people first: smaller numbers come first, the same numbers by
family name.

## Locations

Under **Locations** you add each place with its name, address, phone,
email and opening hours, one line each. For the map enter the
coordinates, e.g. "53.5444, 9.9663": right-click the place in a map to
copy them.

## Drafts

People and locations stay drafts until you choose **Publish**. Changes
to published ones are drafts as well; visitors see them once you
publish again. Only published people and locations can be chosen in the
blocks.

## On the website

- **Team** shows the people with photo, role and how to reach them, of
  all locations or of one.
- **Contact person** shows one person, e.g. beside a contact form.
- **Location** shows one location with address, opening hours and a
  map of OpenStreetMap or Google Maps. The map loads only once the
  visitor agrees, because it comes from another provider. Search
  engines get the address as structured data.

Texts in another language are added with the tabs at the top; without
them visitors see the texts in the first language.
